### Roman Republics
#
# Given explicitly (type = title_type_lr_roman_republic) to the static Roman Republic titles:
#   e_western_roman_republic, e_eastern_roman_republic (01_LR_roman_republic_titles.txt)
#   h_roman_republic (01_LR_roman_restoration.txt)
