# Priorities sit one above every generic entry these cultures could otherwise get, and below any special
# (title/faith/law-keyed) entry that currently wins; where that needs two numbers the entry is split into
# _independent and _vassal versions. de_jure_liege-only regional entries (Britannia, Wales) count as generic.
# clan_government is left out so Muslim clan titles keep winning.
# Admin entries include the dynastic twins (roman/alexandrian_dynastic), per the LR rule that they use admin titles.
